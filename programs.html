01

Promoting financial independence and sustainable livelihoods among persons with disabilities and vulnerable community members.

  • Vocational skills training.
  • Entrepreneurship development.
  • Financial literacy and savings education.
  • Income-generating activities.
02

Promoting equal access to quality, inclusive and accessible education.

  • Provision of learning materials and school uniforms.
  • School-related assistance.
  • Advocacy for inclusive education.
  • Promoting accessible learning environments.
03

Championing the rights, dignity and meaningful participation of persons with disabilities.

  • Disability awareness and rights education.
  • Advocacy against stigma and discrimination.
  • Community sensitisation and inclusion.
  • Promoting equal opportunities and accessibility.
04

Recognising caregivers and providing support systems that improve their well-being and capacity.

  • Mental health and psychosocial support.
  • Caregiver training and education.
  • Peer support networks.
  • Family and community support initiatives.
05

Promoting emotional well-being, resilience, confidence and access to appropriate support.

  • Mental health education.
  • Emotional support and awareness.
  • Confidence-building and mentorship.
  • Counselling and professional service referrals.
06

Promoting informed choices, bodily autonomy, safety and dignity, particularly for persons with disabilities and vulnerable groups.

  • Reproductive health education.
  • Family planning and contraception education.
  • Menstrual hygiene support and dignity packs.
  • HIV/STI awareness and referrals.
  • Consent education.
  • Prevention and awareness of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV).
  • Information on accessing survivor-centred support and referral services.
07

Promoting environmental responsibility and ensuring persons with disabilities participate in climate action.

  • Community cleanups.
  • Tree planting initiatives.
  • Environmental education.
  • Disability-inclusive climate action.
  • Inclusive disaster preparedness.
