OUR STORY

Everyone deserves a chance to thrive.

Her Ability Initiative believes that disability should never be a barrier to dignity, opportunity or participation.

Our organisation works to empower persons with disabilities, caregivers and vulnerable members of the community through education, economic empowerment, advocacy, health awareness and environmental action.

We seek to build communities that recognise abilities, respect human rights and create opportunities for everyone.

Questions about our work?

Who does Her Ability Initiative work with?

Our organisation works with persons with disabilities, caregivers and vulnerable members of the community.

What areas does the initiative focus on?

Our work includes education, economic empowerment, disability rights advocacy, caregiver support, mental health and wellness, sexual and reproductive health, gender-based violence prevention, environmental conservation and climate action.

How can I support the initiative?

You can volunteer, partner with us, support our programs, participate in community activities or share our mission with others.
