WHO WE ARE

Inclusion begins when every voice is heard.

Her Ability Initiative is committed to empowering persons with disabilities, caregivers and communities.

We work to promote equal opportunities, strengthen livelihoods, support education, advocate for disability rights and improve community well-being.
